# APEX INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY & SCIENCE
## PLACEMENT GUIDELINES, INTERNSHIP POLICIES & SCHOLARSHIP SCHEMES (2026-2027)

### 1. CAMPUS RECRUITMENT TRAINING & PLACEMENT ELIGIBILITY
1.1. Placement Eligibility Criteria:
- Minimum overall CGPA: 6.50 without any active backlogs at the commencement of the 7th semester placement season.
- Minimum 85% attendance in mandatory Campus Recruitment Training (CRT) modules covering Data Structures & Algorithms, Aptitude, System Design, and Soft Skills.
- Completion of at least one verified industrial internship of minimum 6 weeks duration.

1.2. Placement Policy (Dream & Super-Dream Offers):
- Tier 1 (Core / Standard Offer): Annual CTC up to $10,000 / Rs. 8,00,000 per annum.
- Tier 2 (Dream Offer): Annual CTC between $10,000 and $20,000 / Rs. 8,00,000 - Rs. 16,00,000.
- Tier 3 (Super Dream Offer): Annual CTC exceeding $20,000 / Rs. 16,00,000 per annum.
- Policy Rule: A student who secures a Tier 1 offer remains eligible to interview for Tier 2 or Tier 3 companies. Once a student receives a Super Dream offer, they are placed in the "Final Placed" category and cannot apply for additional campus drives to allow other students fair opportunities.

---

### 2. INSTITUTIONAL SCHOLARSHIPS & FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE
2.1. Merit-Based Scholarships:
- Chairman's Presidential Fellowship: 100% tuition fee waiver for top 5 rank holders in State/National entrance examinations or students maintaining a CGPA >= 9.75.
- Dean's Merit Excellence Award: 50% tuition fee waiver for students maintaining a CGPA between 9.25 and 9.74 in the preceding academic year.
- Academic Merit Scholarship: 25% tuition fee waiver for students maintaining a CGPA between 8.75 and 9.24.

2.2. Need-Based & Special Category Scholarships:
- Economically Weaker Section (EWS) Grant: Up to 50% tuition waiver for students with certified annual family income below $3,500 / Rs. 2,50,000.
- Women in STEM Leadership Grant: $1,000 / Rs. 50,000 annual grant awarded to top female engineering innovators in Computer Science and Robotics.
- Sports & Cultural National Champions Grant: Up to 75% tuition and hostel fee waiver for students representing the country/state in recognized federations.

2.3. How to Apply:
Scholarship application forms are released on the Student ERP portal every year between August 1st and August 31st. Required verification documents include previous semester grade sheets, family income certificate issued by the Revenue Authority, and bank account details.

### 3. LIBRARY RESOURCES & TIMINGS
3.1. Central Digital Library Working Hours:
- Regular Semester Working Hours: Monday to Saturday from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
- Examination Period Working Hours: Open 24/7 with round-the-clock air conditioning, quiet study zones, and high-speed Wi-Fi.
- Sunday & Public Holidays: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

3.2. Borrowing Privileges:
- Undergraduate Students: 4 books for a loan duration of 14 days (renewable twice).
- Postgraduate Students: 6 books for a loan duration of 30 days.
- Overdue fine: $0.20 / Rs. 10 per day per book after the due date.
