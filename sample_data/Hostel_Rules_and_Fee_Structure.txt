# APEX INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY & SCIENCE
## HOSTEL RESIDENCE POLICIES & ACADEMIC FEE STRUCTURE (2026-2027)

### 1. ANNUAL TUITION & COLLEGE FEES
1.1. Undergraduate B.Tech Programs:
- Computer Science & Engineering (CSE / AI & ML / Data Science): $3,200 / Rs. 1,80,000 per academic year.
- Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE): $2,800 / Rs. 1,50,000 per academic year.
- Mechanical & Civil Engineering: $2,400 / Rs. 1,20,000 per academic year.
- Other Institutional Fees (Library, Laboratory, Internet, Sports): $400 / Rs. 25,000 annually.
- One-time Refundable Caution Deposit: $200 / Rs. 10,000 (payable at time of admission).

1.2. Postgraduate M.Tech & MBA Programs:
- M.Tech (All Specializations): $2,200 / Rs. 1,10,000 per academic year.
- MBA (General / FinTech / Analytics): $3,000 / Rs. 1,60,000 per academic year.

1.3. Fee Payment Deadlines:
- Autumn / Odd Semester Fee: Must be deposited by July 31st.
- Spring / Even Semester Fee: Must be deposited by January 15th.
- Late fee penalty of $5 / Rs. 200 per day applies for the first 15 days of delay. After 15 days, student portal access is temporarily frozen.

---

### 2. HOSTEL ACCOMMODATION & MESS CHARGES
2.1. Hostel Room Categories & Annual Charges:
- Premium Single Occupancy (AC with Attached Bath): $1,600 / Rs. 95,000 per year.
- Double Occupancy (AC with Attached Bath): $1,200 / Rs. 75,000 per year.
- Double Occupancy (Non-AC with Shared Bath): $850 / Rs. 55,000 per year.
- Triple Occupancy (Non-AC with Shared Bath): $650 / Rs. 42,000 per year.

2.2. Mess / Dining Charges:
- Vegetarian & Non-Vegetarian Combined Meal Plan (Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks, Dinner): $900 / Rs. 52,000 per academic year.
- Pure Vegetarian Diet Plan: $800 / Rs. 46,000 per academic year.
- Mess fee is payable in two equal half-yearly installments.

---

### 3. HOSTEL REGULATIONS & CODE OF CONDUCT
3.1. Curfew Timings:
- In-campus hostel entry deadline: 9:30 PM on weekdays (Sunday to Thursday).
- Extended weekend deadline: 10:30 PM on Friday and Saturday with prior digital pass approval.
- Night biometric attendance is conducted strictly at 10:00 PM daily.

3.2. Outstation Night Passes:
Students intending to stay overnight outside the campus or visit hometown must submit an e-pass request through the Student ERP portal at least 24 hours in advance, followed by digital OTP approval from registered parents/guardians.

3.3. Zero-Tolerance Policies:
- Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited under the Supreme Court mandate and leads to immediate expulsion and police reporting.
- Possession or consumption of alcohol, tobacco, narcotics, or prohibited substances within hostel/campus premises results in permanent rustication.
- Electrical appliances such as immersion heaters, induction cookers, and electric irons are not permitted in hostel rooms for fire safety reasons.
