# APEX INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY & SCIENCE
## OFFICIAL ACADEMIC REGULATIONS & EXAMINATION RULES (ACADEMIC YEAR 2026-2027)

### 1. ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENTS
1.1. Minimum Mandatory Attendance:
Every registered student is required to maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in aggregate of all lectures, practical sessions, tutorials, and laboratories conducted in each semester to be eligible for appearing in the End-Semester Final Examinations.

1.2. Attendance Condonation on Medical Grounds:
Students having attendance between 65% and 74.9% may be considered for condonation of attendance shortage by the Academic Dean, strictly on valid medical grounds (hospitalization certificate certified by the Chief Medical Officer) or representation of the College in State/National sports and cultural events. A condonation fee of $50 / Rs. 1,500 per course applies.

1.3. Detainment:
Students who secure less than 65% aggregate attendance shall be detained in that semester and will NOT be allowed to appear for the End-Semester Examination under any circumstances. They must re-register for the entire semester with the subsequent batch upon paying the re-admission fees.

---

### 2. GRADING SYSTEM & EVALUATION SCHEME
2.1. Assessment Split:
- Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE): 40%
  - Mid-Term Test 1: 15%
  - Mid-Term Test 2: 15%
  - Quizzes, Assignments, and Mini-Projects: 10%
- Semester End Examination (SEE): 60%

2.2. Letter Grades and Grade Points:
- O (Outstanding): 90 - 100% | Grade Point: 10
- A+ (Excellent): 80 - 89% | Grade Point: 9
- A (Very Good): 70 - 79% | Grade Point: 8
- B+ (Good): 60 - 69% | Grade Point: 7
- B (Above Average): 55 - 59% | Grade Point: 6
- C (Average): 50 - 54% | Grade Point: 5
- P (Pass): 40 - 49% | Grade Point: 4
- F (Fail / Absent): Below 40% | Grade Point: 0

2.3. Minimum Passing Criteria:
A student must secure at least 40% marks in SEE (24 out of 60) and 40% overall (CIE + SEE) to clear any course.

---

### 3. SEMESTER EXAMINATIONS & ARREARS POLICY
3.1. Examination Schedule:
- Odd Semesters (I, III, V, VII): Theory examinations begin in the 3rd week of November. Practical exams commence in the 1st week of November.
- Even Semesters (II, IV, VI, VIII): Theory examinations begin in the 3rd week of April. Practical exams commence in the 1st week of April.

3.2. Backlog / Supplementary Exams:
Supplementary examinations for backlogs are conducted during the summer break (June-July) and winter break (December-January). A student can register for a maximum of 4 backlog subjects in any single supplementary attempt.

3.3. Revaluation and Script Verification:
Students dissatisfied with their evaluated semester exam answer scripts may apply for Photostat copy verification within 7 days of result declaration upon payment of $15 / Rs. 500 per subject. Formal re-evaluation request can be submitted within 14 days of result declaration.

---

### 4. ADMISSION CRITERIA & DEGREE REQUIREMENTS
4.1. Minimum Duration & Credits:
- B.Tech / B.E. Program: 4 Years (8 Semesters). Minimum total credits required for graduation: 160 Credits.
- M.Tech / M.S. Program: 2 Years (4 Semesters). Minimum total credits required for graduation: 68 Credits.
- MBA Program: 2 Years (4 Semesters). Minimum total credits required for graduation: 72 Credits.

4.2. Honors & Minor Degree Option:
Students with a CGPA >= 8.00 at the end of the 4th semester without any standing backlogs are eligible to register for an Honors degree or a specialized Minor degree by taking 18-20 additional credits across Semesters 5 to 8.
